Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Deb Writes may collect, use and protect information provided through this website.

If you contact Deb Writes through email or a contact form, personal information such as your name, email address and the details of your enquiry may be collected.

Basic website usage data may also be collected through analytics tools.

How information is used

Information provided through this website may be used to:

Personal information will never be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Analytics and tracking

Deb Writes may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the website.

These tools may collect anonymised information including:

  • device and browser information

This information is used to improve the website experience and overall usability.

Data security
