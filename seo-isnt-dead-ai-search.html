SEO Isn't Dead: What Still Matters in the Age of AI Search

Search is changing, but people and search engines still need to find, understand and trust the content on your website.

SEO still helps search engines understand your site

Whenever search changes, the same question comes back: is SEO dead?

I don't think it is. The way people find information is changing, especially as AI answers become more visible. But businesses still need people to find their websites, understand what they offer and decide whether it's right for them.

That's where I think the conversation can get too focused on the latest change in search. A new feature might alter the route someone takes to a website. It doesn't remove the need for a website to make sense once they arrive.

A website still needs to explain what you do

If someone lands on your site, can they work out what your business does and who it helps?

That sounds basic, but it's easy for a website to look polished while leaving those answers unclear. I've seen how much easier it is to understand a business once someone explains it, even though the information is already on the site. Visitors shouldn't have to piece it together for themselves.

Clear wording, useful page headings and links that take people where they expect to go all help. They make the site easier to use and make its content easier to understand.

Content needs to give people something useful

Having content on a website is not the same as giving someone a reason to read it.

A page should answer a real question, explain something clearly or help someone decide what to do next. If it only repeats what can be found everywhere else, the reader may reach the end without knowing what they were meant to take away.

That's one reason I think human thinking still matters in search. As I wrote in Why Human Writers Still Matter in Search, gathering information is only part of the job. Someone still needs to decide what's worth saying and what the reader should leave with.

AI search changes the route, not the need for clarity

AI search can give people another way to discover information, and a business may be mentioned or cited without getting a visit. As I explored in Being Cited by AI Search Is Not the Same as Being Chosen, appearing in an answer is only one part of the journey.

Whether someone arrives through a search result, an AI answer or a link from another page, they still need to understand the business when they reach its website. They need to see why it might be right for them and what they can do next.

So, SEO hasn't become irrelevant. The search results may look different, but a clear website and useful content still give people, and search tools, something they can understand.
