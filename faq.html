What can Deb Writes help with?

I help businesses create clearer content and improve their online visibility. This includes SEO content writing, website copy and AI search support. The right mix depends on your goals and the support you need.

What is SEO content writing?

SEO content writing means creating useful, relevant content that helps people find your business through search engines. It might include website pages, blog articles or service descriptions. The aim is to answer the questions your audience is asking, improve your visibility in search and help the right people take the next step.

What is AI search optimisation?

AI search optimisation focuses on making your business and its content easier for AI-powered search tools to understand and potentially reference. These include tools such as ChatGPT and Google's AI search features. I focus on clear, useful content that explains what your business does, who it helps and why it matters. No one can guarantee that an AI tool will mention or recommend a particular business.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term investment. Results depend on factors such as your website, competition, current visibility and the work involved. Some improvements may appear sooner, but meaningful progress can take several months. I'll focus on the priorities most relevant to your business goals and keep you informed about the work.

Do you work with businesses of all sizes?

I can support businesses with different needs, from improving website content to ongoing SEO and AI search support. The best approach depends on your goals, priorities and the level of support you need.

Can you work with my existing marketing team or agency?

Yes. I can work alongside your marketing team, SEO agency or other suppliers to support your wider strategy. This might include writing content, improving existing website pages or providing extra support where it's needed.

What does a monthly retainer include?

A retainer provides ongoing support shaped around your priorities and agreed in advance. It could include content, SEO or AI search support, depending on the scope. We'll agree what the work involves before the retainer starts.

How much does a retainer cost?

Monthly retainers start at £1,000. The final cost depends on your needs and the agreed scope. I'll confirm what's included before work begins.

Can I book a one-off project?

Yes. One-off projects are available at an agreed hourly rate. I'll confirm the expected work and cost before the project starts.

How do I get started?

Get in touch and tell me a little about your business, what you want to achieve and where you need support. We can then discuss your priorities, the right approach and the scope and cost before any work begins.
