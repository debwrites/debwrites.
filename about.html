ABOUT

I look at what people actually do, not just what a business wants to say.

I'm Deb. I bring a background in occupational therapy to my work in content, SEO and AI search.

Occupational therapy taught me to look at how people think, adapt and manage real situations. What someone intends to do and what they actually do can be very different.

I bring that perspective to business communication: what people need to understand, what might get in their way, and what helps them decide what to do next.

My background is in occupational therapy, where understanding people in their everday environments matters.

That way of looking at things still shapes how I approach every project.

I don't see content, SEO and AI search as separate tasks.

I look at how a business explains what ir does, how people find that information, and whether they can make sense of it when they get there.

That might mean improving a page, looking at how a website guides visitors, or exploring how a business appears in search and AI-generated results. On my Insights page I explore questions about changing AI search results, confusing website journeys and the role of relevant backlinks.

The aim is to make the experience clearer for real people, while helping businesses communicate what they do and are how they are found online.
