Website information

Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how Deb Writes uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and provide information about how visitors use a site.

Like most websites, Deb Writes uses cookies to help improve the user experience, understand how the site is being used and monitor website performance.

Cookies do not harm your device and do not give access to personal files stored on your computer or phone.

How cookies are used on this website

Cookies may be used to understand how visitors use the website, monitor website performance, improve user experience and analyse traffic and page activity.

This helps Deb Writes identify which pages and content are most useful to visitors and where the website can be improved.

This website does not currently use cookies for direct advertising or personalised ad targeting.

Analytics and website tracking

Deb Writes may use third-party analytics tools, including Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, to understand how visitors interact with the website.

These tools may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, scrolling behaviour, clicks and navigation patterns.

This information is used to improve the website experience, content structure and usability.

Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can usually block cookies, delete stored cookies or receive alerts before cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the website function.

Changes to this policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the website, analytics tools or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page.
